How routing works

Triage classifies every inbound case into one of your configured case types, then looks up the row here to pick the downstream route.

When Decision needed? is off, the pipeline emits a stub Decision and dispatches straight to the route (no Decide/Deliver work). Use this for cases that don't need eligibility re-checked — refills, appeals routed to a human queue, obvious non-decidable messages.

Fallback

Tenant rows override the platform defaults. Programs then override tenant rows individually. Any case-type not listed at any level falls through to the platform default.
