Send an application. Get a decision back.

This guide takes you through the real intake path: you email a patient-assistance packet, Verdikt reads it, decides against the program's own rules, writes the reply from a frozen template, and holds it until a person approves. Then the letter comes back to you.

What you need

  • Any email account — Gmail, Outlook, a work address. The sender does not matter.
  • The two PDFs in this folder: ASP-100 and ASP-110.
  • About five minutes, plus however long the reviewer takes to approve.

Step by step · sending

Compose the email

1

Open a new message from any account

Use whatever address you like. Routing is decided by the subject line, not by who sent it, and the decision letter is sent back to the address you used.

2

Address it

To: quire.template.agent@gmail.com
3

Paste the subject exactly

The subject carries two tags. alderwood chooses the pharmacy; [pap:cardiovita-pap-2026] sends it to the decision pipeline and names the program. Without the [pap:…] tag the email is treated as a template upload instead — the most common reason a test seems to do nothing.

Subject: [pap:cardiovita-pap-2026] Alderwood — enrollment for Maria Chen
4

Paste the body

The attachments drive the decision, not the body — but send what a real clinic would.

Hello, Please find attached the completed patient assistance enrollment packet for Maria L. Chen, referral ALD-26-207741. Enclosed: - ASP-100 Patient Enrollment & Prescription Referral (2 pages) - ASP-110 Household Income Worksheet (1 page) The patient is currently uninsured and is requesting assistance under the CardioVita program. Please let me know if anything further is needed. Thank you, Bayview Cardiology Associates

Step by step · what happens next

Wait for a person

6

Verdikt picks it up within a minute

The inbox is checked every 60 seconds. Once your email is in, the pipeline reads both documents, classifies the case, finds the program rules that apply and returns a verdict that cites the rule that decided it. Then it merges that verdict into the program's frozen, human-approved reply template — no language model writes the letter.

7

Nothing is sent until a reviewer approves

Every outcome — approve, deny or missing info — stops in a review queue. A reviewer sees each stage, the rule that fired and the rendered letter, and clicks Approve. This gate is in code, not configuration, so it cannot be switched off by accident.

No reply yet is the system working. If you are waiting on a letter, the case is waiting on a person. Expect it once the reviewer has looked at it, not within seconds.

8

The decision letter arrives in your thread

After approval the letter is emailed back to the address you sent from, as a reply to your original message, with the rendered decision attached.
