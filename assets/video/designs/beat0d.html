Stage I

Ingest

Every channel normalizes into one inbox event and routes to tenant + program.
Extract
A VLM reads fax, postal mail, and email attachments into structured intake slots. Web forms, REST, and MCP arrive as JSON — straight to Retrieve.
Stage II

Triage

Classify the case type; route it accordingly.
Classify
New intake, refill, appeal, missing-info reply, or not-decidable.
Route
Refills → Pharmacy OMS; missing-info → CRM outreach; new intakes → Decide.
Stage III

Decide

The pipeline finds the eligibility rules that apply and reasons over them.
Retrieve
Rules matched by embedding search against a versioned pack.
Reason
Structured verdict — approve, deny, or missing_info. Citations attached.
Stage IV

Deliver

Two artifacts out — the reply and the machine-readable verdict.
Merge
Verdict + frozen template → byte-identical PDF letter.
Deliver
Reply out to the applicant; JSON verdict to downstream systems.
Stage V

Remember

Every artifact preserved and re-verifiable.
Audit
Intake, rules, decision, letter — every stage stamped, hashable, replayable years later.
Rollback
Repin an earlier template version. No re-decision. No drift.
